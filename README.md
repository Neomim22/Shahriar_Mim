NextGen Tech Website
This project is a modern, responsive e-commerce website for "NextGen Tech," a company dedicated to selling cutting-edge technology and gadgets.
Project Overview
The website is designed with a clean, modern aesthetic using the Inter font family and a consistent color palette. It features multiple pages to provide a comprehensive online shopping experience. The site was founded in 2020 by a team of tech enthusiasts.
Key Features
•	Home Page: The homepage serves as a hub for the latest gadgets and features customer testimonials. It includes a prominent hero section encouraging users to explore products.
•	Shop: The shop page is a central catalog for products, with options to filter by category, price range, and brand. It showcases a grid of products, along with a carousel for featured items like the PlayStation 5, Google Chromecast, and Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra.
•	About: The about page tells the story of NextGen Tech, outlines its mission, and introduces the founding team: Vaskar Joshi (CEO & Founder), Md Shahriar Mim (Chief Technology Officer), Yousufur Rahman Tuhin (Lead Product Designer), and DR. Mubashar Ali (Head of Marketing).
•	Contact: The contact page provides a form for users to send messages and lists company contact details, including a general email address (info@nextgentech.com) and a phone number (+44 7570765897). It also lists two physical addresses.
